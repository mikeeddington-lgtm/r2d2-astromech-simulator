padawan360 mod2026 · padawan_secure _mode.ino · generated 2026-07-26 08:50

Two naming systems. CAD name is from the Fusion model (MrBaddeley MK4) and is what the printed part is called. Actuator is the simulator's ID, numbered by position around the droid, front first — so they deliberately do not match: pie0 is MainPie3. Wire to the channel, label the loom with the CAD name, and use the bearing to find it on the droid. Bearing is degrees clockwise from the front.
